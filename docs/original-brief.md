# AI VIDEO STUDIO — WEBSITE, PRODUCT UI/UX & VISUAL DESIGN MASTER BRIEF

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

We are developing an AI-powered video production platform for businesses, initially targeting tourism and hospitality, but designed to support businesses across all industries.

We need to develop two connected but distinct digital experiences:

**A. Public-facing marketing website**
The website that introduces the product, communicates its value, showcases capabilities, attracts customers, explains pricing and supports customer acquisition.

**B. The AI Video Studio application**
The actual software platform customers log into to manage brands, upload and organise footage, create videos, use AI editing tools, manage templates, review outputs and export finished content.

Both experiences must share a cohesive visual identity and design system, but their layouts, navigation and interaction patterns should be designed for their respective purposes.

IMPORTANT: We have not finalised the website sitemap, page structure or complete application information architecture. Do not assume these are already decided.

Your first responsibility is to research, propose and develop the appropriate information architecture and design direction before implementing the full interfaces.

Do not immediately start building pages without establishing the design system and understanding the product requirements.

## B. AI VIDEO STUDIO APPLICATION

This is the logged-in software experience.

It needs to support two connected modes.

### AUTOPILOT

A simple, mobile-first experience for businesses that want to upload footage and let AI do most of the work.

The experience should be approachable for people with little or no video editing knowledge.

Potential workflow:

1. Upload footage.
2. Select a business, brand or experience.
3. Select the content objective.
4. Describe the video.
5. Let AI analyse and select footage.
6. Generate video options.
7. Review and approve.
8. Request changes or export.

Autopilot should feel simple, visual and intuitive.

### STUDIO

A more advanced desktop-first creative workspace for users who want greater control over video production.

It should support:

- Video preview.
- Media library.
- Editing timeline.
- Storyboard.
- AI creative assistant.
- Scene selection.
- Clip trimming.
- Text and graphics.
- Audio and music.
- Captions.
- Brand controls.
- Transitions.
- Colour adjustments.
- Version history.
- Multiple aspect ratios.
- Export settings.

The design should be professional and efficient, with the video itself remaining the visual focus.

We want a hybrid approach: AI does most of the production work, while users can refine the results manually.

Do not attempt to reproduce the complexity of professional software such as Premiere Pro in the initial MVP. Design a powerful but approachable editing experience.

# 11. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

1. Do not create a generic AI SaaS interface.
2. Do not copy the supplied reference websites or software products.
3. Do not select one colour palette and discard the others.
4. Do not assume the website sitemap is already final.
5. Do not confuse the public website with the logged-in application.
6. Do not build a complex editing interface before understanding the product workflows.
7. Do not sacrifice usability for visual effects.
8. Do not use placeholder functionality and claim it is complete.
9. Do not make major architectural changes without explaining why.
10. Do not implement all phases in one uncontrolled development pass.

The final product should feel like a premium creative technology company with a genuinely useful AI video production platform.

We want a product that is visually memorable, professionally designed, intuitive to use and capable of growing into a substantial commercial platform.

Begin with Phase 1. Inspect the repository and produce the design strategy and information architecture proposals before implementing the full design.
