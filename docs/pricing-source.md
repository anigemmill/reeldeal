# 6. Rollover policy

Our selected policy is one-month rollover.

For video and AI generation credits:

- Unused monthly credits carry over for one additional billing cycle.
- Maximum rollover is one month's standard allocation.
- Oldest credits are consumed first.
- Credits expire after the following billing cycle.
- Annual customers receive monthly allowances, not the full year's credits upfront.
- Purchased top-up credits have a proposed 90-day expiry.

Media analysis hours do not roll over.
