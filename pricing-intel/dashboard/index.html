Price-rise break-even

How much volume a price rise can lose before contribution falls. The margin is your assumption: no real cost data has been supplied yet.

Formula: volume retained to break even = margin ÷ (margin + rise). No price elasticity is assumed.

How prices are graded

  • A Official site or menu, current or dated within about 6 months.
  • B Official source, undated.
  • C Third-party, or older than about 6 months. Shown, never in medians unless you include it.
  • Every price links to the page or PDF it was read from, with a quote copied from it.
  • NZ menu prices are GST-inclusive, so all comparisons are GST-inclusive.
  • Sites that refused automated reading were left alone and are listed as gaps.
